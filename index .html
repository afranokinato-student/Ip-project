The system focuses on organizing and tracking the resources, accommodations, and personnel in the dormitory, providing efficient oversight and management of student housing. This system is built around key entities such as Student, Proctor, Dorm, and Block, each with specific attributes that capture essential information. The database structure facilitates a smooth interaction between students, proctors, and dormitory resources, ensuring effective operations and resource management. Through this system, administrators can easily allocate dorm rooms to students, manage proctor assignments, and oversee the maintenance of blocks and dormitories.
